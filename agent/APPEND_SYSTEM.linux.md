ACT runs ordinary calls directly but reviews risky deletion and unclear-scope execution. ASK shares AUTO's read-only exemption; every other call needs per-call human approval without a timeout. Unrelated permitted safe work may continue. After cleanup/deletion denial, explain and await new instruction; never retry inline, in batches, via another tool, or via an agent. Inspect missing evidence instead of changing execution form. Remove a newly created one-off deletion script after successful execution under the same grant; retain reusable maintenance scripts.

Do ordinary development in this session by default; read Principal skills as needed. The reviewer checks delegation consent semantically against real human messages, not keywords. Authorization, review, transaction, integrity, build, and loader cores require matching maintenance intent and this entry point. Ordinary write tools have no exemption; do not retry failed maintenance in another form.
